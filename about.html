Hi, I'm the creator of Panel & Parcel.

I have always enjoyed story telling, illustration, and the way fictional worlds can stay with readers long after they finish a chapter. I created Panel & Parcel to give fans a way to bring pieces of that world into their everyday lives through clothing, artwork, stickers, accessories, and collectibles inspired by an original webtoon.

Every item in the shop is connected to the characters, symbols, and settings of the Fae Realm. My goal is to create merchandise that feels like a natural extension of the story instead of simply placing a logo on a product.

Our Purpose

Panel & Parcel is a place for readers who enjoy fantasy stories and want to celebrate the characters they connect with. The shop is designed to make fan merchandise easy to browse while keeping the artwork and story at the center of the experience.
